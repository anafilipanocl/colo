Sometimes we just need to be held.

Professional cuddling is a space for platonic, consensual human touch — a place where you can feel safe, slow down and simply be.

Touch is part of being human

Maybe you live alone. Maybe you’re far from the people you love. Maybe you’re going through something difficult. Maybe you’ve spent a long time being the person who holds everyone else.

Or maybe nothing is particularly wrong — you just want to share the joy of human touch.

You don’t need to earn it, explain it or have a reason that feels important enough.

What this space is

Professional cuddling is fully platonic. It isn’t dating, massage, therapy or a sexual service.

We are two human beings sharing space and touch, with boundaries that are spoken about rather than assumed.

There’s no right way to be here, and nothing you need to perform.

  • You can talk. You can be quiet. You can laugh.
  • You can feel awkward.
  • You can ask for something and then realise you don’t actually want it.
  • You can change your mind.

Questions

Nothing here is a strange question. If yours isn’t answered, write to me and ask.

General
What is professional cuddling? +

Professional cuddling is a space for platonic, consensual human touch. We might cuddle, hold hands, sit close, talk, laugh or simply rest together. There is no right way for a session to look. We find what feels comfortable together.

Is it sexual? +

No. Professional cuddling is fully platonic and we both remain clothed throughout the session. Sexual touch isn’t part of this space. Clear boundaries are what allow us to relax into closeness without wondering where it is supposed to lead.

Is this therapy? +

No. Although my background is in psychology and therapeutic work, cuddling sessions are not therapy. My background does shape the care I bring to the space — particularly around safety, consent, trauma awareness and making room for different bodies, identities and ways of being.

What is Colo? +

Colo is one of those Portuguese words that doesn’t quite translate. Dar colo is to hold someone, to offer warmth, comfort, and care. That feeling is at the heart of what I hope to create here.

Who is welcome? +

Adults of different genders, sexualities, bodies, abilities, identities and ways of relating are welcome here. You don’t need to fit a certain profile or have a particular reason for coming. You can simply be you.

Safety
Where do sessions happen? +

Sessions take place in a quiet, private space in Lisbon. I’ll share the exact address once we’ve spoken and arranged a session.

Is it safe? +

Feeling safe can mean different things to different people, so I don’t want to assume what it looks like for you. My aim is to create that space together.

We talk beforehand, check in before any touch, and keep communicating as we go. Boundaries are spoken rather than assumed, and you can say yes, no, not yet, something different, or change your mind at any time.

We both stay fully clothed, and there is nothing you have to do or perform while you’re here.

Sessions
What happens before my first session? +

First, send me a message. Then we’ll have a short, free intro call so we can meet, talk through any questions and get a sense of whether this feels right for both of us.

What actually happens during a session? +

We start by checking in and talking about what feels comfortable that day. From there, we take it slowly. We might talk, sit close, cuddle, laugh, be quiet or simply rest together. There’s no checklist and nothing you have to do. In the end there’s always a time to integrate.

What if I feel nervous or awkward? +

That’s okay. Sharing touch with someone new can feel vulnerable, unfamiliar or just a little strange at first. We can take our time. Awkwardness is allowed here too.

Do I need to know what I want? +

Not at all. You might know exactly what kind of touch feels good to you, or you might have no idea yet. We can find out together.

What should I wear? +

Something clean, comfortable and easy to relax in. Basically, whatever you’d happily curl up on a sofa in. We both stay fully clothed throughout the session.

How long are sessions? +

Sessions are available for 60, 90 or 120 minutes. If it’s your first time, we can figure out together what feels like a good place to start.
